Know Your Market Movers

Every day, news headlines move the stock market up or down. Investors have to react in real time. Can you predict which way the market goes? Some are obvious. Some will surprise you.

🐂 Bull Market
Prices are rising. Investor confidence is high. Economy looks strong. "Bullish" means optimistic about the market going up.
🐻 Bear Market
Prices are falling 20%+ from a recent high. Fear is spreading. "Bearish" means pessimistic — expecting prices to drop.
The Fed
The Federal Reserve controls interest rates. Raising rates usually hurts stocks. Cutting rates usually helps stocks. It's the most watched institution on Wall Street.
Earnings
Every quarter, companies report profits. If they beat expectations → stock goes up. If they miss → stock drops. Expectations matter as much as reality.
