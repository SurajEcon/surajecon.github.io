What Is a Credit Score?

> A number between 300 and 850 that tells lenders how risky you are to loan money to. A higher score = lower interest rates = thousands saved over your lifetime. These five factors build it.

Payment History
35%
The most important factor. Paying on time every month builds your score. A single missed payment can drop your score 50-100 points and stays for 7 years.
Credit Utilization
30%
How much of your credit limit you use. Keep it under 30%. Using 90% of your limit signals financial stress to lenders — even if you pay it off.
Credit History Length
15%
Older accounts help your score. This is why closing your oldest credit card hurts you — even if you don't use it.
Credit Mix
10%
Having different types of credit (cards, loans, mortgage) shows you can manage various financial products responsibly.
New Credit (Hard Inquiries)
10%
Every time you apply for a new credit card or loan, lenders do a "hard pull" on your credit. Too many inquiries in a short time signals financial desperation and drops your score slightly.
