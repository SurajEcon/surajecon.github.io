📈 What is "to the Moon"?
Crypto slang for a coin's price rising dramatically and fast. "BTC is mooning!" means it's surging. Comes from the idea of a rocket leaving Earth's atmosphere.
📉 What is a "Crash"?
A rapid, severe price drop — often 30-80% in days or weeks. Crypto crashes are more extreme and frequent than stock market crashes. They can wipe out years of gains overnight.
🔀 What Moves Crypto?
Government regulation, celebrity tweets, exchange hacks, macroeconomic fear, Bitcoin halvings, and pure speculation. There is no earnings report — only sentiment.
⚠️ The Hard Truth
Studies show most retail crypto traders lose money. The people who make money are early adopters and those who dollar-cost average and hold through crashes.
