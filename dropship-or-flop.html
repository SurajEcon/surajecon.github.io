Know What Makes a Winner

Dropshipping = selling products online without holding inventory. You list it, someone buys it, your supplier ships it. Sounds easy — but most products fail. Can you spot the winners?

💰 Profit Margin
You need at least 30% margin to cover ads, returns, and fees. Under 20% and you're working for free — or losing money.
🚚 Shipping Time
Customers expect fast delivery. Products shipping from China in 3-6 weeks will kill your reviews. Under 2 weeks is the target.
🏪 Competition
If Amazon already sells it cheap, you can't compete. Look for products that are hard to find in stores but have real demand online.
🎯 Ad Potential
Can you show this product in a 15-second video that makes people stop scrolling? Visual, demonstrable products sell on social media. Boring ones don't.

🎓 The 4 Golden Rules of Dropshipping

💰Aim for 30%+ profit margin minimum. Less than that and fees, ads, and returns will eat you alive.
🚚Shipping under 2 weeks is critical. Long shipping = bad reviews = dead store.
🏪Avoid anything Amazon dominates. You'll lose on price every single time.
🎯The best products are visually demonstrable — something you can show off in a 15-second video that makes people think "I need that."
