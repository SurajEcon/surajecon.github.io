Market Machine

Chapter 2, Markets and the Economy. Two neighboring towns, a trade deal to strike, a price to discover, and a loop of money to trace. Nothing here is a quiz. You run the towns, drag the sliders, and watch the market do what markets do.

🏘️ Two towns, two goods. Rivertown and Laketown each split their workers between bread and furniture. Their trade-offs are different, and that difference is the whole point.
💱 Trade changes what's possible. On their own, each town is stuck on its own frontier. Trade at the right rate and both towns end up with more than they could make alone.
