NMT Summer STEM 2026 — Outreach Course

Money Hacks:
Engineering Your Financial Future

📅 June 2026 — Monday through Friday ⏰ Mon–Thu 9am–noon, 1–4pm  |  Fri 9am–noon 👤 Instructor: Dr. Suraj Ghimire 🎓 High school students (NMT Summer STEM)

A weeklong intensive on personal finance and financial literacy for advanced high school students. Students leave with a completed Personal Financial Blueprint, a side-hustle business plan, and the vocabulary to make smarter financial decisions — starting now.

Week Schedule

Mon–Thu you have 9am–noon and 1–4pm. Friday is 9am–noon only. Walk students to Fidel lunch room after each morning session.

Mon Money & Mindset — Where does your money go?
9:00–9:30
Introductions + Ice Breaker
"What would you do with $1 million?" — round-the-room, no wrong answers. Sets the tone.
9:30–10:00
Mini-Lecture: Income, Expenses, Net Worth
The three numbers that define your financial life. Keep it concrete — use real salaries for jobs students might want.
10:00–10:45
🃏 Budget Battleground (In-Person Card Game)
Each student draws a Life Card (job, city, salary). Allocate your monthly income across expense categories using play money. Whoever has the most left over wins. Debrief: who went broke on rent?
10:45–11:00
Debrief + Discussion
What surprised you? Which expenses were bigger than expected?
11:00–11:45
💻 Spend or Save? (Online Game)
Ten rounds of real-life decisions that compound. Students play individually on phones.
▶ Open Game
11:45–12:00
Introduce the Financial Blueprint
Hand out the printed blueprint. Students fill Section 1 this week and take it home Friday.
12:00–1:00
Lunch — Walk students to Fidel
1:00–1:30
Mini-Lecture: The Psychology of Money
Why smart people make dumb financial decisions. Anchoring, social pressure, lifestyle creep.
1:30–2:30
🃏 The Keeping Up Game (In-Person Card Game)
Each student is a character with a peer group that keeps upgrading. Decide each round: match or save? Play money tracks balances. Who has money left at age 30?
2:30–3:30
Blueprint Work — Section 1
Students write their own estimated budget at age 22: what job, what city, what will things cost?
3:30–4:00
Share Out
Each student shares one thing that surprised them today.
Tue Make Your Money Work — Investing & Markets
9:00–9:15
Recap Monday
9:15–9:50
Mini-Lecture: How Investing Works
Stocks, index funds, the market. Keep it simple: you're buying a tiny piece of a company.
9:50–10:30
💻 The Time Machine: Compound Interest
Students move sliders to discover what happens when they start at 18 vs 28. The moment the number collapses is the whole lesson.
▶ Open Calculator
10:30–11:00
📊 Launch Stock Market Simulation
Each student gets a fictional $10,000. Pick 3 real companies using Yahoo Finance on their phones. Record starting portfolio on the printed tracker. Check back Thursday.
11:00–11:45
💻 Bull or Bear?
News headline drops. Does the market go up or down? Competitive — first to 7 correct wins.
▶ Open Game
11:45–12:00
Blueprint Section 2 Intro
12:00–1:00
Lunch
1:00–1:30
Mini-Lecture: Index Funds vs. Picking Stocks
Why most experts can't beat the market, and what that means for regular people.
1:30–2:30
🃏 Fund Manager Showdown (Card Game)
Two teams each get 10 company description cards (no names). Pick 5 to invest in. Reveal the real companies and their 5-year returns. Who picked better?
2:30–3:30
Blueprint Work — Section 2
Investing plan: when to start, how much per month, what to invest in.
3:30–4:00
Open Q&A
Wed Build Something — Entrepreneurship, Dropshipping & Crypto
9:00–9:45
Mini-Lecture: How Businesses Make Money
Revenue, costs, profit margin. Real examples: lemonade stand → app → dropshipping store.
9:45–10:30
Shark Tank Lite — Round 1 Prep
Each student picks a side-hustle idea (tutoring, reselling, lawn care, social media). Start filling the one-page business plan template.
10:30–11:00
Mini-Lecture: What is Dropshipping?
Walk through a real example live: AliExpress → Shopify → order → fulfillment. Pros, cons, realities.
11:00–11:45
💻 Dropship or Flop?
Ten products. Is it a viable dropshipping product or a total flop? Students evaluate margin, shipping, competition, and ad potential.
▶ Open Game
11:45–12:00
Blueprint Section 3 Intro
12:00–1:00
Lunch
1:00–1:30
Mini-Lecture: What is Crypto? What is Blockchain?
Use the "shared Google Doc" analogy. Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins. Hype vs. reality.
1:30–2:15
💻 Crash or Moon?
Real historical crypto charts with the last 3 months hidden. Draw where you think it goes, then see what actually happened — and why it was nearly impossible to predict.
▶ Open Game
2:15–2:30
Honest Debrief: Crypto as Speculation vs. Investment
Stories of people who made and lost fortunes. What's the difference between investing and gambling?
2:30–3:30
Blueprint Work — Section 3 (Side Hustle Plan)
3:30–4:00
Preview Thursday: Shark Tank Lite pitches tomorrow afternoon
Thu Debt, Credit & Pulling It Together
9:00–9:30
📊 Stock Portfolio Check-In
Students look up their stocks from Tuesday. Who's up? Who's down? Why? Introduce idea that markets are hard to predict even for professionals.
9:30–10:15
Mini-Lecture: Taxes, Credit Scores & Debt
The three things nobody teaches you but everyone needs to know.
10:15–11:00
💻 Credit Score Simulator
Eight decisions across eight years. Watch your score shift in real time. At the car loan stage, students see in dollars what their current score actually costs them.
▶ Open Game
11:00–11:45
🃏 Debt Avalanche vs. Snowball (Card Game)
Two groups, four debt cards. One group uses avalanche method, one uses snowball. Calculate total interest paid. Which wins mathematically? Which feels better?
11:45–12:00
Blueprint Section 4 Intro
12:00–1:00
Lunch
1:00–2:15
🎤 Shark Tank Lite — Pitches
Each student gives a 3-minute pitch. Others are "investors" with 5 play-money chips each. Dr. Ghimire plays skeptical shark (one tough question per pitch). Whoever raises the most funding wins.
2:15–3:15
Blueprint Final Polish — Sections 1–4
3:15–3:45
💻 Financial Future Flashforward
Five big life choices — education, housing, car, investing, career. Watch your projected net worth at 40 change with every decision.
▶ Open Game
3:45–4:00
Preview Friday: Blueprint presentations tomorrow morning
Fri Your Financial Future Starts Now
9:00–9:20
📊 Final Stock Check
Who ended the week up? Celebrate the winner. Why did some stocks move the way they did?
9:20–10:00
Blueprint Presentations
Each student shares one section (2–3 min). No grades, no pressure — just sharing what they built.
10:00–10:30
The 5 Moves You Can Make This Year
Open a savings account. Track spending for 30 days. Start one side hustle experiment. Learn one investing concept per month. At 18: open a Roth IRA.
10:30–11:00
💻 Final Boss Quiz — Team vs. Team
15 questions, 5 categories, streak bonuses. Everything from the week. Play money prize for the winners.
▶ Open Quiz
11:00–11:50
Closing Circle + Certificates
Each student says one thing they will do differently with money. Dr. Ghimire shares briefly — growing up in Nepal, what money meant, what he wishes he'd known.
11:50–12:00
Photo, Goodbyes
