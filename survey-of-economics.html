Survey of Economics

One semester,
both halves of economics

A single-semester introduction to both microeconomics and macroeconomics for non-majors, built around David A. Anderson's Survey of Economics (Worth Publishers / Macmillan Learning, 1st edition). Each chapter pairs the reading with a short browser game so a concept gets tested by doing something with it, not just by answering questions about it.

How the Games Work

🧪
Simulation, not a quiz
Each game is built around the chapter's core mechanism, something you manipulate and watch respond, rather than a list of questions with one right answer.
🖥️
Runs in any browser
No installation and no account. Each game is a single page that works on a laptop, Chromebook, or phone.
📸
Screenshot for credit
When you finish, the results screen shows your score. A screenshot of that screen is what gets uploaded for the chapter assignment.
🔁
Replayable
Every game can be replayed from scratch, the numbers and targets stay the same, so a second attempt is a chance to actually understand the concept rather than memorize an answer key.
