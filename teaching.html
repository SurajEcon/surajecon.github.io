New Mexico Tech — CS & Engineering

Teaching economics
to engineers

I teach graduate and undergraduates at a Hispanic-Serving Institution in Socorro, NM. My goal is to make economic reasoning concrete through real data, applied problems, and browser-based games students can play in class.

Principles of Microeconomics
Supply and demand, market equilibrium, consumer and producer theory, externalities, and public goods. Applied examples drawn from energy and agricultural markets.

GDP, unemployment, inflation, fiscal and monetary policy, the Phillips Curve, and open-economy macroeconomics. Strong emphasis on understanding Federal Reserve decisions.

Classroom Games

All games run in the browser — no installation, no accounts. Designed for ~30 minutes of class time. Each is built around one economic concept that's hard to make concrete through lecture alone.

🏛️

Macroeconomics

Chair the Fed

Students role-play as Fed Chair, setting interest rates each quarter to manage inflation and unemployment. Makes the Phillips Curve tradeoff visceral before they formalize it.
